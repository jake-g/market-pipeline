## Executive Summary: The Year in Review

2024 was defined by a **prolonged bull market** and significant **macro-political realignments**. The primary driver was the **Artificial Intelligence (AI) investment surge**, which propelled major indices to record highs despite high interest rates and global instability [1, 2].

*   **Market Performance:** The **S&P 500 surged ~23.8%**, marking its second consecutive year of 20%+ gains, a feat not seen since 1997-1998 [2]. The **Nasdaq Composite jumped nearly 30%**, significantly outperforming the blue-chip Dow Jones, which rose 13% [3].
*   **The "Magnificent Seven" Impact:** Tech giants (Alphabet, Amazon, Apple, Meta, Microsoft, Nvidia, and Tesla) accounted for **over 50% of the S&P 500’s total returns** [3]. Nvidia (NVDA) emerged as the centerpiece of this trend, cementing its position as the third-largest global company by market cap [1, 4].
*   **Structural Tech Shifts:** AI transitioned from experimental interest to an **integral enabler of business operations**, converging with IoT and automation to drive smarter workflows [5, 6].
*   **Macroeconomic Catalysts:** Performance was supported by **cooling inflation**, resilient consumer spending, and the Federal Reserve’s eventual pivot toward **interest-rate cuts** [2, 7, 8].
*   **Geopolitical and Domestic Turmoil:** The year saw the **reelection of Donald Trump** to a nonconsecutive second term and continued conflict in **Gaza and Ukraine**, which exacerbated global trade disarray [1, 9, 10].

**Q3: Political Realignment and Climate Records**

*   **Macro Event:** **July was the hottest month ever recorded**, validatng climate change concerns as temperatures breached the 1.5°C threshold [18-20].

**Q4: The Trump Reelection and Crypto Surge**
*   **Defining Moment:** **Donald Trump defeated Kamala Harris** on November 5 [1, 10].

*   **Macro Event:** **Hurricane Helene and Hurricane Milton** caused catastrophic damage across the U.S. [1]. The Fed signaled a shift in its 2025 rate-cut path, causing a minor late-December "air pocket" in stock prices [17].
