## Executive Summary: The Year in Review

2022 was defined by a transition from pandemic-era stimulus to a high-inflation, high-interest-rate regime, resulting in the worst annual performance for U.S. equity benchmarks since 2008 [1, 2].

*   **Dominant Macro Catalysts:** Inflation reached 40-year highs, peaking at **9.1% (CPI)** in June, driven by stimulative policies, supply chain disruptions, and the energy shock following **Russia's invasion of Ukraine** [3-5].
*   **Federal Reserve Pivot:** The Fed executed its most aggressive tightening cycle since 1981, raising the federal funds rate by **425 basis points** across seven meetings to reach 4.4% by year-end [1, 6, 7].
*   **Market Performance:** U.S. equity markets entered a formal bear market [8]. Growth-style leadership ended abruptly as **Big Tech lost nearly $4 trillion** in valuation; conversely, value stocks outperformed growth by over 20 percentage points [7, 9].
*   **Structural Shifts:** The 40-year downtrend in interest rates ended as 10-year Treasury yields broke out to the upside [10, 11]. Global negative-yielding debt plummeted from $18.4 trillion in 2020 to less than $25 billion by end-of-year 2022 [12].
*   **Tech Trends:** While the "Metaverse" dominated early 2022, the year ended with a massive pivot toward **Generative AI** following the releases of DALL-E 2 and ChatGPT [13-15].
