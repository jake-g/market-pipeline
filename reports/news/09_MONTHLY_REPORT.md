With 10-year Treasury yields setting 24-year highs, corporate borrowing costs have adjusted higher across all credit tiers. While cooling core PCE prints provide the Federal Reserve leeway to pause benchmark rate hikes, elevated long-term risk-free rates expand the Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC), depressing terminal value assumptions in Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) models across long-duration growth assets [2, 4].

Consequently, corporate capital expenditure is bifurcating along strict ROI thresholds. Discretionary software and enterprise SaaS firms face ongoing valuation multiple compression as elevated discount rates demand immediate margin execution over long-term growth stories [20, 49]. Conversely, AI hardware infrastructure capex remains highly inelastic. Capital deployment is expanding past standard GPU acceleration into custom silicon IP—exemplified by Amazon's $1B+ Synopsys deal—and advanced chipmaking equipment from toolmakers like Applied Materials and KLA Corporation [5, 6, 39, 40]. Furthermore, expanding Thermal Design Power (TDP) in next-generation AI clusters requires a physical transformation of data center power topologies. Enterprise operators are actively transitioning to **800V DC architectures** and direct-to-chip liquid cooling systems—benefiting specialists like **nVent Electric** and **Vertiv**—to prevent severe step-down energy losses and thermal throttling inherent in traditional AC power and air cooling architectures [15, 23].

In the energy and defense sectors, structural capital allocation will remain insulated from interest rate friction. In energy, nuclear buildout financing is decoupling from traditional regulated utility rate-base investments. Hyperscalers are increasingly utilizing direct power purchase agreements (PPAs)—such as Amazon’s $3B+ Maryland commitments—to fund off-grid and dedicated nuclear infrastructure, ensuring rapid project execution [11]. Combined with GE Vernova’s historic NRC permit victory for its BWRX-300 SMR, advanced nuclear is shifting from theoretical planning to physical site preparation [9, 10]. Simultaneously, sovereign defense mandates are directing long-term capital toward autonomous warfare hardware and naval fleet overhauls, cementing structural order backlogs for primary contractors including **Boeing**, **Huntington Ingalls Industries**, and **Kratos Defense** through 2027 [12, 17, 24, 25].
