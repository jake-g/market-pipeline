## Executive Summary: The Year in Review

2020 was characterized by **unprecedented volatility**, encompassing the worst global pandemic in a century and the fastest bear market in history followed by a shocking recovery [1-3]. The S&P 500 plunged 34% in early spring only to surge **65% from its March low**, finishing the year up nearly 14% [4-6].

*   **Primary Catalysts:** The COVID-19 pandemic decimated global demand and industrial output, leading to the first-ever negative oil futures prices in April [7-9]. Massive intervention by the Federal Reserve, which slashed rates to near-zero, and a **$2.2 trillion federal stimulus package** provided the liquidity necessary for a rapid market rebound [4, 10, 11].
*   **Structural Shifts:** The "new normal" accelerated the digital transformation, as nationwide stay-at-home orders forced a wholesale change in how work is conducted [12-14]. Heavyweight technology companies led the market recovery, with growth stocks outperforming value by the **widest margin in decades** [15-17].
*   **Tech Trends:** 2020 saw the rise of massive language models, most notably **OpenAI’s GPT-3** with 175 billion parameters, and the expansion of AI tools into industrial infrastructure and project management [18-21].

*   **Defining Moments:** Market hit record highs in mid-February (DJIA 29,551.42) before the sudden onset of the global pandemic [22-24].

### Q2: Economic Decimation and the Tech Pivot
*   **Defining Moments:** U.S. GDP plummeted by a record **31.4%**, and the economy lost **20.8 million jobs** in April alone [26-29].
*   **Market Reaction:** Technology giants led a "V-shaped" recovery; the Nasdaq Composite reached a new all-time high by June [15-17].
*   **Macro Event:** Racial unrest following the death of George Floyd sparked worldwide protests, adding to domestic political turmoil [30-32].

### Q3: Reopening and Resilience
*   **Defining Moments:** Real GDP rebounded with a **33.4% growth rate** as businesses began reopening safely [33-35].
*   **Market Reaction:** Speculative fervor increased with the dominance of retail day traders; however, a September correction saw the S&P 500 fall 9.6% in three weeks [36-38].
*   **Macro Event:** The **Abraham Accords** were signed, establishing diplomatic ties between Israel, the UAE, and Bahrain [30-32].

### Q4: Vaccines and the Election
*   **Defining Moments:** Joe Biden won the 2020 Presidential Election with a record 66.7% voter turnout [39-41].
*   **Market Reaction:** Positive vaccine efficacy data from Pfizer and Moderna in November pushed the Dow Jones to break **30,000 for the first time** [42-44].
*   **Macro Event:** A second $900 billion pandemic relief bill was passed in December, providing further stimulus checks and unemployment benefits [45-47].
